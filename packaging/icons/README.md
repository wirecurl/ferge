# ferge icons

The mark is a lowercase f whose stem crosses a wave: ferge is the ferryman.

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `../linux/icons/hicolor/` | The app icon (dark tile) for the freedesktop icon theme, installed to `share/icons/hicolor/` and named after the app ID: `scalable/apps/com.wirecurl.ferge.svg`, PNGs from 16 to 512 pixels, and `symbolic/apps/com.wirecurl.ferge-symbolic.svg` in one colour on a 16 px grid, recoloured by the desktop. The app also sets the 128 px PNG as its X11 window icon. |
| `../macos/` | `ferge.icns` for the app bundle, with the tile inset on the canvas as macOS expects, and its source `ferge-macos.svg`. |
| `variants/` | Tiles in dark (the app icon), accent and light, and the mark without a tile: on dark, on light, plain white, plain black. |

Colours: tile `#0d1215`, edge `#232b31`, stem `#f2f5f4`, wave `#49e0bd`. On light
backgrounds: `#f3f4f1`, stem `#0b0e0f`, wave `#006b55`.
